Terms for care home profiles

These terms apply when a care home claims and manages its free profile on Care Home Hearing.

Who can claim a profile

Only someone authorised to manage the home's public information, such as the registered manager, a deputy manager, the owner or the nominated individual. We check every claim by phoning the home on the number the Care Quality Commission (CQC) lists for it.

What you publish

  • Answer the hearing care questions honestly. Your page labels this information as coming from the home.
  • Your own words must be about hearing care and must not advertise, include links or contact details, or name residents or staff.
  • Do not include any information about residents.
  • Keep your profile up to date. You can change or unpublish it at any time.

What we may do

  • Hide your own words if they break these terms. Your facts stay visible.
  • Look into concerns that families, staff or others raise about your page.
  • Remove a verified badge if the evidence behind it was not true.
  • Remove access for anyone who should no longer manage your profile.

Things that never change

  • Your page always shows the facts we receive from CQC, including your CQC rating, with its date and a link to CQC's report.
  • Independent verification is not a CQC rating, and the badge must not be described as one.
  • Results are ordered by distance or name, never by who pays.
  • A home can be verified without buying any Hear4All service, and buying one does not help it pass.

Who runs Care Home Hearing

Care Home Hearing is created and funded by Hear4All, a hearing care provider. About Care Home Hearing · Privacy notice
