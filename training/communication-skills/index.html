Module 6

Communication Skills in Care Homes

Practical techniques that help residents with hearing loss follow conversations, with real-life examples from personal care, medication rounds, mealtimes, visits, activities, emergencies and end-of-life care.

About 21 minutes Everyone who talks with residents, including care staff, nurses, kitchen and housekeeping staff, activities coordinators and volunteers

Start module Printable summary

What you will learn

By the end of this module you will be able to:

  • Gain a resident's attention and position yourself so they can see and hear you
  • Speak clearly and naturally without shouting, and rephrase rather than repeat
  • Reduce background noise, improve lighting and allow extra time
  • Check understanding respectfully
  • Adapt communication for residents living with dementia and hearing loss
  • Use writing, pictures, gestures and captions when they help
  • Apply these skills in daily care, group settings, emergencies and end-of-life care
