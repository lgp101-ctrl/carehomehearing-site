What earwax is and why it matters

Earwax is a natural, protective substance. It usually looks after itself, but some older people are more likely to have it build up.

Earwax is normal

Earwax (its medical name is cerumen) is made by glands in the outer part of the ear canal. It:

  • traps dust and dirt before they reach the eardrum
  • keeps the skin of the ear canal moist and protected
  • helps protect against infection

The ear canal is self-cleaning. Tiny movements of the skin, and movement of the jaw when we talk and eat, slowly carry wax towards the entrance of the ear, where it dries and falls out. Most people never need earwax removed.

Why build-up is more common in care homes

Earwax is more likely to build up in older people because:

  • wax tends to become drier and harder with age
  • the ear canal may have more hair
  • hearing aids, earmoulds and earplugs can stop wax moving out naturally and push it deeper
  • some people naturally make more wax, or have narrow ear canals
  • previous attempts to clean the ears with cotton buds may have pushed wax further in

When earwax becomes a problem

Earwax only becomes a problem if it builds up and blocks the ear canal, or stops hearing aids from working. Then it can:

  • muffle hearing (a type of conductive hearing loss)
  • cause a blocked or full feeling, discomfort or tinnitus
  • block hearing aids or make them whistle
  • stop a professional examining the ear or taking ear impressions for new earmoulds

Your role

Care staff do not remove earwax. Your role is to notice possible signs, keep hearing aids clean, record what you see, and ask for a professional ear examination when needed. The rest of this module explains how.

Check your understanding

Your answers are not recorded anywhere except, if possible, on this device.

Which statement about earwax is correct?

Earwax protects and cleans the ear canal and usually works its way out naturally. It only needs professional attention if it builds up and causes problems.

Why are hearing aid users more likely to have earwax build up?

Anything worn in the ear canal for many hours a day can block the natural movement of wax out of the ear and push it further in.
