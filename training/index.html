Free training for care staff

Training Academy

8 short modules for care staff, nurses and volunteers. No previous knowledge needed. Each takes about 10 to 20 minutes and ends with a short assessment.

How it works

  • Free, with no sign-up needed. Start any module in any order.
  • Read short lessons and answer quick questions as you go.
  • Pass the final assessment (80% or more) to download a certificate. You can retry as often as you like.
  • Your progress is saved on this device.

All modules (about 2.5 hours in total)

  1. 1

    Module 1: Understanding Hearing Loss

    How hearing works, why hearing changes with age, how to spot hearing loss in residents and when to ask for a hearing assessment.

    About 16 minutes 5 lessons

  2. 2

    Module 2: Deaf Awareness

    Understand the different ways people experience deafness and hearing loss, respect each resident's communication preferences and avoid harmful assumptions.

    About 18 minutes 5 lessons

  3. 3

    Module 3: Earwax Management

    What earwax is, how it affects hearing and hearing aids, what care staff can safely do, and when to get a professional ear examination. This module teaches observation and escalation only, not wax removal.

    About 20 minutes 5 lessons

  4. 4

    Module 4: Hearing Aid Management

    Daily care of NHS and privately supplied hearing aids, from telling left from right and putting them in, to cleaning, charging, storage and preventing loss.

    About 20 minutes 6 lessons

  5. 5

    Module 5: Hearing Aid Troubleshooting

    A simple, safe way to work out why a hearing aid is not working, what you can check, what you must not attempt, and who to contact – including the interactive troubleshooter.

    About 19 minutes 5 lessons

  6. 6

    Module 6: Communication Skills in Care Homes

    Practical techniques that help residents with hearing loss follow conversations, with real-life examples from personal care, medication rounds, mealtimes, visits, activities, emergencies and end-of-life care.

    About 21 minutes 5 lessons

  7. 7

    Module 7: Assistive Listening Technology

    When and how to use personal amplifiers, remote microphones, TV listeners, hearing loops, group listening equipment, speech-to-text and captions – for residents with and without hearing aids.

    About 19 minutes 5 lessons

  8. 8

    Module 8: Referral and Escalation

    How to recognise hearing and ear problems, decide whether they are routine, urgent or an emergency, and escalate them safely in line with NICE and NHS guidance.

    About 19 minutes 5 lessons

For Hearing Loss Champions and managers

After the core modules, the Hearing Loss Champion programme helps designated staff lead hearing care in their home.

This website gives general information and training. It does not replace advice from a GP, nurse, audiologist or other health professional. If someone is seriously ill or injured, or shows signs of a stroke, call 999. For urgent advice, contact their GP or NHS 111.
