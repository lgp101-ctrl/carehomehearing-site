Professional services from Hear4All

Hear4All is a hearing care provider. It created and funds Care Home Hearing. If your care home would like hands-on professional support, these are the services Hear4All offers.

Before you enquire

  • Hear4All works alongside the NHS and does not replace NHS hearing care. Residents can continue to use NHS services, and we will always signpost to them.
  • All services are subject to confirmation by Hear4All, including availability in your area.
  • Any costs are explained clearly before you agree to anything. The initial consultation is free.
  • Not for emergencies. For urgent or emergency problems, call 999 or contact the GP or NHS 111.

Online enquiries open soon. Until then, start with the NHS and the services you already use: see where to get help.

Services for care homes

  • Care home hearing screening day

    A hearing care professional visits to check residents' ears and hearing, with consent, and gives you a clear summary for care plans. Anyone who needs NHS or medical follow-up is signposted.

  • Ear examination and professional earwax removal

    Examination of the ear canal and, where clinically appropriate and with consent, earwax removal by a trained professional, for example by microsuction.

  • Hearing assessments

    Hearing assessments for residents, carried out in the care home wherever possible.

  • Hearing aid maintenance and optimisation

    Checking, cleaning, retubing and fine-tuning hearing aids. For NHS hearing aids, we help you work with the NHS service that supplied them.

  • Hearing aid fitting and aftercare

    For residents who choose private hearing care, fitting and ongoing aftercare. We always explain NHS options too.

  • Assistive listening solutions

    Advice on TV listeners, personal amplifiers, hearing loops and other equipment for residents and communal areas.

  • On-site staff training

    Practical, hands-on training for your team that builds on the free online modules.

  • Hearing care improvement programme

    Support to act on your Hearing-Friendly Care Home self-assessment, including Hearing Loss Champion support.

Prefer to start with the free resources?

Everything in the Training Academy, toolkit and self-assessment is free, whether or not you use Hear4All's services.

This website gives general information and training. It does not replace advice from a GP, nurse, audiologist or other health professional. If someone is seriously ill or injured, or shows signs of a stroke, call 999. For urgent advice, contact their GP or NHS 111.
