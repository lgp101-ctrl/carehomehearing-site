How independent verification works

Every care home on Care Home Hearing shows one of three statuses for its hearing care. Only an independent visit can give a home the top one.

The three statuses

Not yet shared
The home has not shared its hearing care on Care Home Hearing yet. That does not mean it gives poor hearing care: ask on your visit.
Shared by the home
The home has shared information about its hearing care. It has not been independently checked.
Independently verified
An independent assessor visited the home and checked its hearing care against the published standard. It lasts 12 months, and the date it ends is shown on the home's page.

Not a CQC rating. Verification is separate from the Care Quality Commission. Each home's page also shows its CQC rating, with a link to CQC's report.

The Hearing Friendly Care Home badge

Independently verified homes can display the Verified badge for 12 months, on their website, brochures and in reception. Homes taking part in the free Hearing Friendly Care Homes campaign use the Member badge, which does not mean the home has been assessed.

The stars on the Verified badge are decoration. Verification is pass or fail, not a rating. To check a badge is current, look for the home on Care Home Hearing.

The visit

  1. The care home asks for a visit from its account.
  2. We agree the price and a date with the home.
  3. An independent assessor spends about half a day at the home. They look at hearing aids, records and the environment, and talk to staff.
  4. The assessor records each criterion as met, not met or not applicable, and writes a report for the home.
  5. If the home meets the standard, its page shows "Independently verified" for 12 months. If not, nothing changes publicly, and the home gets the report to help it improve.

Who assesses

Assessors are independent of the homes they visit. They are not paid by results and earn no commission on Hear4All sales. An assessor cannot verify a home they have worked for, or sold to, in the last 12 months, and they declare this before every visit.

Keeping it independent

  • A home can be verified without buying any Hear4All service, and buying one does not help it pass.
  • Results are ordered by distance or name, never by who pays.
  • Anything paid for is labelled.
  • The standard is published in full and free to read. A small advisory panel reviews it every year.
  • Families and staff can raise a concern about any page, and a badge can be removed if the evidence was not true.
  • There are no reviews or star ratings.

Care Home Hearing is created and funded by Hear4All, a hearing care provider. These rules keep verification separate from its services.
