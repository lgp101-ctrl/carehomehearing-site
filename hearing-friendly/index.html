Hearing-Friendly Care Homes

Free tools for managers and Hearing Loss Champions to make good hearing care part of everyday life in your home: daily hearing aid checks, good communication, a calmer environment and timely referrals.

  • Self-assessment

    Check seven areas of hearing care and get a personalised improvement plan. About 10 minutes.

  • Environment audit

    Walk round your building and find quick wins for noise, lighting, seating and safety.

  • Hearing Loss Champion programme

    An advanced pathway for staff who will lead hearing care in your home.

  • Implementation plan

    A practical 90-day plan for managers, from appointing a Champion to embedding routines.

  • Manager dashboard

    Track your assessments, plan and staff training on this device.

  • Claim your free profile

    Show families choosing a care home how you look after residents' hearing.

  • The Hearing-Friendly Care Home standard

    The open standard: 33 criteria in seven areas, and what an assessor looks for.

  • Independent verification

    How a visit works, who assesses, and the rules that keep it independent.

About self-assessment

These tools help you review and improve your own practice. They are not an independent assessment, inspection, accreditation or certification, and this website does not award any "hearing-friendly" status.

Need outside help?

See where to get help, including your local NHS audiology service.

This website gives general information and training. It does not replace advice from a GP, nurse, audiologist or other health professional. If someone is seriously ill or injured, or shows signs of a stroke, call 999. For urgent advice, contact their GP or NHS 111.
