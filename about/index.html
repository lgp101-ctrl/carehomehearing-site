About this academy

Care Home Hearing is a free learning platform about hearing care for people living in UK care homes. It is for care staff, nurses, managers, volunteers and families.

Who runs it and who pays for it

Care Home Hearing was created and is funded by Hear4All, a hearing care provider. We think it is important that you know this.

Hear4All set up the academy because hearing loss in care homes is common and often overlooked, and because care staff told us they wanted practical training they could use without feeling sold to. Everything on this site is free, and most of it can be used without registering.

Our editorial rules

  1. No product or brand promotion in learning content. Training modules, Quick Help, family pages and toolkit resources do not promote Hear4All or any other company or product.
  2. NHS first. We always point to NHS and existing services first. We never suggest that private hearing care is automatically better than NHS care, or that NHS care should be replaced.
  3. Safety first. Urgent and emergency problems are always directed to 999, NHS 111, the GP or the care home's own escalation policy.
  4. Clinical review. All clinical content must be reviewed and approved by a qualified audiologist. Each module shows its review status and references.
  5. Commercial content is separate and labelled. Information about Hear4All's own services is only on clearly labelled pages, such as Professional support.
  6. Your data is not used for sales without your permission. Registering for a certificate does not add you to a marketing list. We only send updates to people who tick the separate marketing box.
  7. No endorsement implied. We refer to guidance from organisations such as NICE, the NHS, RNID and Care England. This does not mean they endorse or are connected with this academy.

Certificates

Learners who pass a module assessment can download a Care Home Hearing training completion certificate, created by Hear4All. These certificates show that you completed our training. They are not externally accredited, CQC-approved or CPD-accredited.

Clinical content

Our content is original and written for UK care homes, informed by national guidance. You can see the review status and references for every module on our clinical review and references page.

This website gives general information and training. It does not replace advice from a GP, nurse, audiologist or other health professional.

Reporting a mistake

If you think something on this site is wrong or out of date, please tell us using the contact details on our privacy notice.
