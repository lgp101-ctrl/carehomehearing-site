Clinical review and references

How we make sure our content is safe and accurate, and where it comes from.

How content is written and reviewed

  1. Content is written in plain English for care settings, informed by national guidance such as NICE guidelines and NHS information, and by care-sector guidance.
  2. Every module is reviewed by a qualified audiologist before it is marked as approved, and reviewed again at least every two years or when guidance changes.
  3. Care staff are never taught invasive procedures, such as removing earwax or putting instruments in the ear, or how to diagnose.
  4. Urgent and emergency advice follows NHS and NICE guidance and always directs people to NHS services.
  5. Device procedures always defer to the manufacturer's instructions and the resident's care plan.

Until a module has been approved, it shows "Clinical review: pending". This lets you see exactly where each module is in the process.
