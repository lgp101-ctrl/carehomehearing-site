Questions to ask a care home about hearing care

A printable card of twelve questions for families to take on a care home visit.

Download PDF

Take this card on your visit. Tick each question when you have asked it, and note the answer.

  • Who checks and cleans residents' hearing aids each morning?
  • How do you find out about a new resident's hearing?
  • What happens if a hearing aid stops working or goes missing?
  • Do you keep spare hearing aid batteries, and who orders more?
  • How do you help residents get hearing tests and repairs?
  • How are your staff trained in hearing care?
  • Does someone in the home lead on hearing care?
  • How do staff talk with residents who find it hard to hear?
  • Is there a quiet place for visits and conversations?
  • How do you look after ear health and earwax?
  • How will you keep us informed about my relative's hearing?
  • Do you have TV subtitles, a hearing loop or other listening help?
