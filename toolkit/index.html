Hearing Care Toolkit

Free printable checklists, forms, guides and posters. Each one links back to its online version with a QR code.

Resident information

Some templates are for recording information about residents. Download and print them, or copy them into your care home's approved systems. Never enter resident information on this website. Store completed copies securely in line with your data protection policy.

  • Checklist Holds resident information

    Daily hearing aid care checklist

    A one-page weekly grid for checking a resident's hearing aids every day. Tick each check, or write your initials.

    For: Care staff PDF

  • Routine

    Morning and evening hearing aid routine

    A simple step-by-step routine to display in staff areas or keep with the resident's care plan.

    For: Care staff PDF

  • Checklist

    Weekly hearing care checklist

    A weekly check for each unit or floor, usually completed by the Hearing Loss Champion or senior on duty.

    For: Hearing Loss Champions and senior carers PDF

  • Form Holds resident information

    Resident hearing care passport

    A one-page summary of a resident's hearing needs, equipment and communication preferences, to keep with the care plan and send with them to hospital.

    For: Care staff, residents and families PDF

  • Guide

    Hearing aid troubleshooting guide

    A one-page guide to the four-step check and who to contact, for staff rooms and medication trolleys.

    For: Care staff PDF

  • Guide

    Earwax awareness guide

    What care staff can and cannot do about earwax, the signs to look for, and the warning signs that need urgent help.

    For: Care staff PDF

  • Form Holds resident information

    Hearing concern referral form

    An internal form for care staff to record a hearing or ear concern and pass it to the nurse, manager or GP, using a structured SBAR format.

    For: Care staff, nurses and managers PDF

  • Form Holds resident information

    Hearing aid loss incident form

    Record a lost hearing aid, the search, who was told and what will change to prevent it happening again.

    For: Care staff and managers PDF

  • Audit

    Care home hearing environment audit

    A printable walk-round audit of lounges, dining areas, bedrooms, quiet spaces, reception and safety. You can also complete it online for an instant action plan.

    For: Managers and Hearing Loss Champions PDF

  • Poster

    Communication tips poster

    Eight simple tips for talking with residents who have hearing loss. Print it for staff rooms, nursing stations and lounges.

    For: Everyone who talks with residents PDF

  • Checklist

    Family hearing care checklist

    Things to check during visits and questions to ask at care reviews, for families and friends of residents with hearing loss.

    For: Families and friends PDF

  • Handbook

    Hearing Loss Champion handbook

    A practical guide for staff taking on the Hearing Loss Champion role, with responsibilities, routines, a monthly plan and useful records.

    For: Hearing Loss Champions and managers PDF

  • Guide

    Questions to ask a care home about hearing care

    A printable card of twelve questions for families to take on a care home visit.

    For: Families choosing a care home PDF

This website gives general information and training. It does not replace advice from a GP, nurse, audiologist or other health professional. If someone is seriously ill or injured, or shows signs of a stroke, call 999. For urgent advice, contact their GP or NHS 111.
