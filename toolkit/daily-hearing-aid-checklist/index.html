Checklist · For care staff

Daily hearing aid care checklist

A one-page weekly grid for checking a resident's hearing aids every day. Tick each check, or write your initials.

Download PDF

Confidential once completed. Complete on paper or in your care home's approved systems, and store securely in line with your data protection policy. Never enter resident information on this website.

Resident (initials or room number, as your policy allows)
Week beginning
Hearing aids: left / right / both · type · supplier and phone
Tick or initial each check every day
Check MonTueWedThuFriSatSun
Hearing aids in by agreed time, right aid in right ear
Switched on and working (new battery or full charge)
Clean – wiped, microphones brushed
Tube, dome or wax filter clear – no wax or water
Comfortable – no soreness, redness or discharge
Taken out at night, cleaned and stored in labelled case
Battery door open overnight, or aids on charger (switched on)

If something is wrong: follow the four-step check – Power, Path, Placement, Person. If it still does not work, contact the supplier. Pain, discharge or a sudden change in hearing: take the aid out of that ear and get medical advice the same day (GP or NHS 111).

General information only. Always follow your care home's policies, the manufacturer's instructions and the resident's care plan. In an emergency call 999. Version: November 2026.
