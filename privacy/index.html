Privacy notice

We collect as little personal information as possible. You can use almost all of this site without telling us who you are.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Care Home Hearing (carehomehearing.org) is run by Hear4All (full legal name to be confirmed), which is the data controller for the personal information described here.

Never enter resident information

This website does not collect information about care home residents. Please do not type resident names, health details or other personal information about a resident into any form on this site. Printable templates in our toolkit, such as the hearing passport, should be completed on paper or in your care home's own approved systems.

Using the site without registering

  • Your training progress (lessons completed and quiz scores) is saved only in your own browser's storage on your device. It is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Usage statistics. We count how often things happen, such as a module being started or a resource being downloaded, so we can improve the site and report on how it is used. These counts are anonymous totals per day. We do not store IP addresses, and we do not use cookies, fingerprinting or third-party analytics.

We do not use cookies on the public website. The private administration area uses one essential session cookie for staff who sign in.

Optional registration

Registration is optional. If you choose to register, we collect your name, email address, care home and job role, and we store your training progress and completions so that you can continue on another device and so that we can keep a record of completed training.

  • Lawful basis: your consent. You can withdraw it and ask us to delete your record at any time.
  • Marketing: we only send you updates or information about Hear4All's services if you tick the separate marketing box. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Retention: we keep registration records while you use the academy and delete them after 24 months without activity, or sooner if you ask.

Professional enquiries

If you send an enquiry about Hear4All's professional services, we collect the details in the form: care home name, organisation, postcode, contact name, job role, work email, phone number, number of residents and the service you are interested in. We use this to reply to your enquiry and arrange any services you request.

  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Marketing: only if you tick the separate marketing box.
  • Retention: 24 months after our last contact, unless your organisation becomes a customer, in which case we keep records as required for that relationship.

Linking your training to your care home

If you are registered, you can choose to share your training with the care home where you work. Only if you tick the box, the home's managers see your name, job role and the modules you have passed. The home's public page shows only the number of trained staff, never your name. If the home marks you as a Hearing Loss Champion, your first name and initial appear on its page only if you choose to show them.

  • Lawful basis: your consent. You can stop sharing at any time on the My care home page, and it takes effect straight away.

Care home accounts

When a care home claims its free profile, we collect the name, job role, work email address and phone number of the person claiming it, and of any colleagues they invite. We use these to check the claim (by phoning the home on the number the Care Quality Commission lists for it), to send sign-in links and to contact the home about its profile and any verification.

  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in keeping care home profiles accurate and secure.
  • Retention: claims for 24 months; accounts until 24 months after the last sign-in; verification records for 6 years after the verification ends, so badges can be audited.

Finding a care home

You can search, shortlist and send messages to care homes without giving us any details. We never store what you search for. Your shortlist is kept in your own browser. When you use "Ask this home", you send the message yourself; we only count, without any details about you, that someone asked.

When you search by postcode, we look up where that postcode is using postcodes.io, a free service built on open data from the Office for National Statistics. The postcode is sent to postcodes.io and kept in a cache for 30 days, with no link to you.

If you tell us about a problem with a care home's page, we keep your message, and your email address if you give it, for 24 months.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site, such as our web hosting company and, for enquiries, our customer records and email systems. They act on our instructions and must keep it secure.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access your information, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent. To use these rights, contact us using the details above.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Security

Forms are sent over an encrypted connection. Stored information is kept on a secure server with access limited to authorised staff.

Read about optional registration

This website gives general information and training. It does not replace advice from a GP, nurse, audiologist or other health professional. If someone is seriously ill or injured, or shows signs of a stroke, call 999. For urgent advice, contact their GP or NHS 111.
