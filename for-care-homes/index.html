Everything here is free: hearing care training for your team, quick answers during a shift, and a profile that shows families how you look after residents' hearing.

Your home's free profile

Free profiles for every care home in England are coming soon, so families can see how you look after residents' hearing. Until then, train your team and use the self-assessment.

Professional support

For hearing tests, earwax removal or hearing aid repairs, start with the NHS and the services you already use. The guide also explains private options, including Hear4All's.

Where to get help

This website gives general information and training. It does not replace advice from a GP, nurse, audiologist or other health professional. If someone is seriously ill or injured, or shows signs of a stroke, call 999. For urgent advice, contact their GP or NHS 111.
